# Code of conduct

Scripture Tools brings together people who love the Bible and who often read it
differently. That is a gift, and it needs care. This code describes how we
treat one another in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the
project meets.

## What we ask of everyone

- **Be kind and assume good faith.** Disagree with ideas, never with people.
- **Respect every tradition.** Contributors come from many churches and
  backgrounds. Don't mock, belittle or try to convert anyone, and don't use the
  project to promote a church, ministry or cause.
- **Keep to the work.** Discussions here are about making the tools accurate
  and useful, not about settling doctrine. If a conversation turns into a
  debate about what a passage means, step back to the question we can answer:
  what does the text say, where, and who proposed this reading?
- **Cite, don't insist.** When you think something is wrong, point to the verse
  or the source. Evidence is welcome; pressure is not.
- **Be patient with newcomers.** Everyone was new once, to code or to the
  Bible.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks
- Discriminatory remarks about anyone's race, ethnicity, nationality, sex,
  disability, age, appearance or faith
- Unwelcome sexual language or attention
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Repeatedly derailing discussions after being asked to stop

## What happens when it goes wrong

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock discussions, and
block people who don't follow this code, in proportion to what happened. We'll
explain the decision when it's safe and useful to do so.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer at
**[plate.collage5r@icloud.com](mailto:plate.collage5r@icloud.com)**. Reports are kept confidential.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space, and when someone is representing the
project elsewhere.
